## exile-leveling

Repository: <https://github.com/HeartofPhos/exile-leveling>

Exile-UI acknowledges that its default guide and PoB-import concepts were originally derived from exile-leveling. ExileQuesting studied exile-leveling's typed route/fragments model as prior art but does not bundle its implementation.

## XileHUD

Repository: <https://github.com/XileHUD/poe_overlay>

XileHUD is GPL-3.0-only. It was reviewed to understand established user expectations around PoB import, Client.txt area-ID tracking, overlay modes, passive-tree progression, clipboard item parsing, and release/update UX. No XileHUD source code or data is copied into ExileQuesting's MIT-licensed implementation.
